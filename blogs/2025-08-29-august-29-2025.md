Feature Speech-to-Text has just launched chirp_3 in Public Preview. With this Public Preview, Chirp 3: Transcription we are now expanding language transcription in more than 85+ languages and locales, in addition to StreamingRecognize and SyncRecognize requests for real-time and short-form audio. Under the chirp_3 model flag, you can experience significant improvements in accuracy and speed, and leverage powerful features like Speaker Diarization and language-agnostic transcription.
